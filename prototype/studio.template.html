Arrangement

Drag across bars in a lane to draw a clip. Drag a clip to move it, drag its right edge to make it longer (the pattern repeats). Drag along the bar numbers to set a loop, click them to jump.

Piano roll

Click to add a note, drag it to move, drag its right edge to change length, click it again to delete. Shift+click makes a note softer. Space play, ⌘/Ctrl+D duplicate clip, Delete remove clip.
