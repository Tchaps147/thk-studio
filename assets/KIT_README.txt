TECH HOUSE SYNTH KIT (v3)
30 kicks, 30 bass one-shots and 55 drum sounds, all synthesized from scratch (oscillators, FM, wavefolding,
resonant filters, saturation). No samples were used, so they're yours to use freely.

Format: 44.1 kHz, 24-bit, mono WAV, peak-normalized to -1 dBFS.

TIPS
- Long sustaining basses (Reese, Sub Sustain, Wobble, Filter Swell) work best in Simpler with Classic mode
  and a short release, so the note ends when you let go of the key.
- Put closed and open hats in the same choke group in a Drum Rack so a closed hat cuts the open one.
- For a rumble: send 03, 04 or 09 to reverb then distortion, low-pass around 200 Hz, sidechain to the dry kick.
